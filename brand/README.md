Web-ready derivatives are generated by `scripts/build-brand.mjs` into `public/`.

```bash
nvm use && node scripts/build-brand.mjs
```

## The strategic convergence worth noting

The mark contains a **bridge**, with a road running over it, inside a compass
ring. The Phase-0 positioning — arrived at before any of this artwork was
seen — is *"Navadisha is the bridge between Nepal's education system and its
industry."*

The identity and the strategy independently landed on the same idea. That is
unusually lucky and worth using deliberately: the bridge motif should carry the
positioning throughout the site rather than sitting decoratively in the corner.

## Three findings on the artwork

None of these block launch. All three get more expensive to fix later.

### 1. The flat version is the better logo, and it is trapped in a banner

The lockup in the top-left of `asset-3.png` is markedly stronger than the 3D
version: clean navy and orange, readable silhouette, no bevels or gradients. It
is extracted for reference as `flat-lockup-REFERENCE.png` and
`flat-mark-REFERENCE.png`.

It is only available as a **raster crop from a marketing banner**, on a light
background, at limited resolution. It cannot be placed on a dark background, and
it will soften on retina displays.

**Recommendation:** have the flat mark redrawn as **vector (SVG)**. It is a few
hours of work for an illustrator and it is the difference between an identity
that works everywhere and one that works in one place.

### 2. The 3D mark does not survive reduction

At 64px the 3D version reads as a blue blob; the compass points, bridge and road
all collapse. At 32px — the favicon size, and the size buyers actually see most
often — almost nothing is legible.

Automated alpha-keying of the flat version was attempted and **abandoned**: the
key is luminance-based, and the brand orange is bright enough that it gets
removed along with the background. There is no clean automated path from the
banner crop to a transparent asset. This needs the vector redraw.

**Interim:** the header pairs the mark image with the wordmark set as **live
text** in Manrope ExtraBold, so the type stays sharp at every size and adapts to
dark mode. Only the small mark is bitmap.

### 3. The artwork says "Pvt. Ltd." and carries a ™

Company registration is still in progress, so neither is accurate yet:

- **"Pvt. Ltd."** asserts an incorporated private limited company exists. The
  site therefore renders *"Navadisha Consulting & Innovations"* without the
  suffix until the certificate is issued — see the note in `src/data/company.ts`.
- **™** signals a trademark claim. Unregistered-mark usage varies by
  jurisdiction, but pairing it with an unregistered company name is worth a
  lawyer's five-minute opinion rather than a designer's assumption.

**Recommendation:** commission the vector redraw in **two variants** — one with
the "Pvt. Ltd." descriptor for use after incorporation, one without for now.
Same file, one text layer different.

## Palette

Client-supplied anchors, with ramps built around them in `src/styles/tokens.css`.

| Role | Hex | Token anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Primary — Navadisha Blue | `#0A4D8C` | `--color-base-600` |
| Secondary — Navadisha Orange | `#F57C00` | `--color-edu-500` |
| Technology accent — Teal | `#00A6A6` | `--color-tech-500` |
| Dark | `#0F172A` | `--color-neutral-900` |
| Light | `#F8FAFC` | `--color-neutral-50` |

**⚠ The orange and teal fail text contrast on a light background** — `#F57C00`
lands at ≈2.8:1 and `#00A6A6` at ≈2.9:1 against `#F8FAFC`, where 4.5:1 is
required. They are correct as fills, borders and graphic accents, and wrong as
body text.

The token layer handles this: `--pillar-edu` and `--pillar-tech` point at the
darker `-700` steps (≈5.6:1 and ≈6.1:1) for text, while `--pillar-*-fill` keeps
the true brand hex for fills. **Do not "simplify" this by pointing the text
tokens at the brand hex** — that is the accessibility regression this structure
exists to prevent.

## Typography

Self-hosted subset WOFF2 in `public/fonts/`, generated by the same script. No
third-party font CDN: it removes a DNS lookup and TCP connection on the
high-latency mobile connections this site is built for, and keeps a clean
privacy posture for NGO and international clients.

| Role | Face |
|---|---|
| Display / headings | **Manrope** 400–800 |
| Body / UI | **Plus Jakarta Sans** 400–800 |
| Devanagari | **Noto Sans Devanagari** 400–700 |

The brief listed Manrope as primary and Plus Jakarta Sans as secondary. That was
read as *Manrope for display* (more distinctive, closest to the logo wordmark)
and *Jakarta for body* (wider apertures, better at small sizes on Android). If
the intent was the reverse, swap two lines in `tokens.css` — nothing else
changes.

Only the two Latin faces are preloaded. The Devanagari file is 121KB and is not
needed above the fold, so preloading it would compete with the hero for
bandwidth on exactly the connections being optimised for.
