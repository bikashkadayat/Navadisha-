## S3 — The Bridge

**Eyebrow** — `Our position`

**H2**
> Education, technology and industry operate separately. The cost of that
> separation is carried by everyone.

**Lead**
> Institutions prepare people for work. Employers need capability they can use.
> Technology is now the medium through which both operate. In Nepal these three
> rarely meet by design — and where they do not, students lose direction,
> institutions lose relevance, and industry loses the people it needs.

**Five nodes**

| Node | Line |
|---|---|
| Education | Schools, colleges and universities carrying responsibility for what comes next. |
| Technology | The systems and digital capability institutions increasingly depend on. |
| Innovation | New methods and new tools, and the discipline to apply them well. |
| Industry | Employers who need people able to do the work. |
| Opportunity | What becomes available once the other four are connected. |

**Closing line**
> Navadisha works across all five. That is the practice.

## S5 — How We Work

**Eyebrow** — `Method`

**H2**
> Four stages. The fourth is the one that matters.

**Lead**
> Most engagements end when a deliverable is handed over. Ours end when your
> team no longer needs us.

| # | Stage | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Diagnose | We establish what is actually wrong before proposing anything. Data, interviews, observation. No recommendations at this stage. |
| 02 | Direct | A prioritised path, sequenced and costed, that you can take to a board and defend line by line. |
| 03 | Deliver | We implement the priorities we agreed, in the order we agreed them. |
| 04 | Enable | Documentation, training and handover. Your team runs it without us. That is the point. |
