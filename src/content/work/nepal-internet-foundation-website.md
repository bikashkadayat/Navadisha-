Nepal Internet Foundation works on internet access and digital inclusion in
Nepal. Its programmes, initiatives and partnerships had no single public home.

We designed and deployed a responsive public website with a scalable content
architecture and brand-aligned UI/UX — structured so the Foundation's own team
can publish and maintain content independently, without returning to a
developer.

The result is a public-facing site that presents the organisation's work and
makes its information accessible to partners, funders and the communities it
serves.
